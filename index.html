ROBOT LEARNING & AUTONOMOUS DRIVING

Hello, I'm Ziheng.

I am a master's student at the Global Institute of Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Tong Qin. I received my bachelor's degree in Vehicle Engineering from Beijing Institute of Technology.

My research focuses on end-to-end policy learning and Sim-to-Real transfer. I build interactive digital twins of real scenes and use them to train and evaluate robot policies. I also work on vision-language-action models for manipulation and camera-based end-to-end autonomous parking.

I interned with Xiaomi's Embodied AI Division from April to August 6, 2026. I will join Zhuoyu (卓驭) on September 21, 2026. Before graduate school, I led the BIT Formula Student Driverless team and worked on autonomous racecars.

I am interested in end-to-end algorithms for autonomous driving and robotics. For research discussions or opportunities, please get in touch.
